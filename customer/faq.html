FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about shopping with VÊLORA.

HOW CAN WE HELP YOU?

Find answers about your account, orders, payments, delivery and shopping experience.

01 — ORDERS & SHOPPING

How can I place an order?

Select a product, choose your size and quantity, add it to your bag and proceed to checkout. Enter your delivery details and select a payment option.

Where can I view my orders?

Visit the My Orders page to view your order history and available order status.

Can I cancel an order?

Contact our customer support team as soon as possible to request an order cancellation.

Do I need an account to shop?

You can create a VÊLORA account to manage your profile, view your orders and enjoy a more personalised experience.

How can I save products for later?

Use the wishlist heart option on a product to save it to your wishlist for later.

How can I find products by category?

Visit the Shop page and select Men, Women, Kids or Maternity to explore products in that category.

02 — ACCOUNT & PROFILE

How can I create a VÊLORA account?

Visit the Registration page, enter your name, email address, phone number and password, then submit the registration form.

Can I update my personal information?

Visit the My Profile page to enter or update your personal information and delivery address.

What should I do if I have trouble signing in?

Check that your email address and password are entered correctly. Contact support if you need further assistance.

How can I access my wishlist?

Click the wishlist icon in the header to view your saved products.

03 — PAYMENTS

Which payment methods are available?

Our checkout interface provides Cash on Delivery, UPI and Credit/Debit Card options.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Cash on Delivery is included as a payment option in our project demonstration.

Are online payments secure?

The current project demonstrates payment method selection. Actual payment processing has not been connected.

04 — SHIPPING & RETURNS

How much is the delivery charge?

Delivery is free on orders of ₹1,999 or more. A ₹99 delivery charge applies to smaller orders in our demonstration.

How can I track my order?

Open the My Orders page to check the latest order status available.

How can I request a return?

Contact our customer support team for assistance with return-related questions.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery information is displayed with the available products in our shopping interface.
